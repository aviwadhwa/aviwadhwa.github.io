01

Post-Newtonian dynamics in (A)dS and dual CFT data

How does the AdS/CFT dictionary describe the most familiar gravitational system: two massive bodies bound by a nearly Newtonian potential? How do relativistic corrections modify the orbital dynamics and dual field theory data, and what role does gravitational radiation play?

In this project we study the post-Newtonian dynamics of an inspiralling binary in Anti de-Sitter and de-Sitter background. Using AdS/CFT we then extract information about the dual boundary field theory (CFT). In dS, this framework provides a systematic route to understanding how cosmological curvature affects binary dynamics and gravitational-wave signals.

02

Soft theorems and gauge invariance

Soft theorems describe infrared properties of gauge theories. How do they get modified when the gauge invariance is non-trivial: as in the case of Chern–Simons gauge theories?

We studied soft theorems for gauge theories with Chern-Simons perturbations in both flat and curved (de Sitter) background.
